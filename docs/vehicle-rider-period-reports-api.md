# Vehicle and rider assignment period reports

Both endpoints accept `fromDate` and `toDate` in `yyyy-MM-dd` format. The dates are inclusive calendar dates in Riyadh time (UTC+03:00). Both require `reports.read`, `fleet.assignments.read`, `fleet.vehicles.read`, and `riders.read` permissions.

| Method | Route | Grouping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/api/reports/fleet/vehicle-assignments?fromDate=2026-09-01&toDate=2026-09-30` | Every vehicle, with its rider assignments in start-time order |
| GET | `/api/reports/fleet/rider-assignments?fromDate=2026-09-01&toDate=2026-09-30` | Each person who actually drove, with vehicles used in start-time order |

An assignment appears when any of its time overlaps the requested period. Each row gives its original `startedAtUtc` and `endedAtUtc`, the clipped `periodStartedAtUtc` and `periodEndedAtUtc`, `daysInPeriod`, and `totalAssignmentDays`. Days are elapsed 24-hour periods rounded to four decimal places; for example, 10 complete days are `10`, and 12 hours are `0.5`. An open assignment is counted through the response's `asOfUtc` time, never beyond it. A completed assignment's `totalAssignmentDays` uses its full recorded start-to-end interval, even if the report only shows part of it.

The vehicle report includes every vehicle and returns `totalAmountToCollectInPeriodSar`, the sum of that vehicle's displayed rider assignment charges. Each assignment includes `vehicleType`, `monthlyCostSar`, `dailyCostSar`, and `costInPeriodSar`, using the same calculation as the rider report. Vehicles without overlapping assignments have an empty `assignments` array, `totalDaysAssignedInPeriod: 0`, and `totalAmountToCollectInPeriodSar: 0`. Gaps between assignments contribute no charge. The amount is due for the recorded use in the selected period and does not subtract payments already received.

The rider report also returns `totalVehicleCostInPeriodSar` for each rider. Its assignments include `vehicleType`, `monthlyCostSar`, `dailyCostSar`, and `costInPeriodSar`. Cars (`vehicleType=2`) cost SAR 1,800 per fixed 30-day month (SAR 60/day); motorcycles (`vehicleType=1`) cost SAR 800 per fixed 30-day month (SAR 800/30 per day). These rates apply to any requested range, including months with 28, 29, or 31 calendar days. Cost uses the exact elapsed duration of the clipped assignment, including fractional days, rather than the rounded `daysInPeriod`. Each assignment's amount is rounded to two decimal places, with midpoint rounding away from zero; the rider total sums those rounded amounts. Daily rates are not rounded before calculation. Days without an assignment have no cost. For example, 6 days with a car cost SAR 360.00, a 10-day gap costs nothing, and 14 days with a motorcycle cost SAR 373.33, giving a rider total of SAR 733.33.

Vans, trucks, other vehicles, and unrecognized vehicle types have no specified rate: their monthly, daily, and period costs are `null`. If any assignment in a rider or vehicle row has no rate, its total amount is also `null` so an incomplete total cannot be mistaken for the full cost. A vehicle with no overlapping assignments returns a zero total even if its type has no specified rate. The priced assignments remain available individually. Costs follow the report's existing actual-driver grouping.

Both reports sum individually rounded assignment amounts, so their totals reconcile for the same period and `asOfUtc`. For example, two one-day motorcycle assignments cost SAR 26.67 each and produce a vehicle total of SAR 53.34.

Each assignment includes both the assigned rider profile and the actual driver. When `isRealRider` is `true`, `actualRiderId`, `actualRiderName`, and `actualRiderIqamaNo` come from the assigned rider profile and its employee. When `isRealRider` is `false`, those fields come from the separate `RealRider` record, while `assignedRiderProfileId` and `assignedRiderName` continue to show the registered profile. The rider report groups the same person across assignments by Iqama number, including separate `RealRider` rows created during vehicle switches. If a non-real assignment has no `RealRider` record, it appears under an `unidentified:` rider key with no actual-driver name, rather than being attributed to the assigned profile.

For example, if CAR-A was with rider A from September 1 at midnight until September 11 at midnight, then with rider M from September 11 until September 16, the vehicle report lists two rows of 10 and 5 days, with charges of SAR 600 and SAR 300, a vehicle total of 15 days, and `totalAmountToCollectInPeriodSar: 900`. The rider report shows 10 days and SAR 600 for A, and 5 days and SAR 300 for M.
